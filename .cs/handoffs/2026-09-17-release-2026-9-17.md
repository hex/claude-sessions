## 5. Key Technical Concepts

- The statusline render's cost is **process births**, not commands run: a
  command substitution and each side of a pipeline are processes too
  (`docs/statusline.md` "Data sources and performance", corrected this
  conversation).
- Under the sidebar bridge, Claude Code spawns the bridge, not cs-statusline:
  ~6 of the 9 births per tick are the bridge's own (mkdir lock, sh, mv, rmdir,
  pipe subshells) and cannot be removed from inside cs.
- `grep -q` exits at the first match, so a writer piping into it dies of
  SIGPIPE and `pipefail` turns a MATCH into a failure. A here-string is a file,
  not a pipe, so there is no reader to close.
- The integrate lock (`<git-dir>/cs/integrate.lock`) is taken by BOTH
  `cs -integrate-feature` (which records its pid) and the autosave hook (which
  records nothing).
